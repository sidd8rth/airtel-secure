What we hear from BFSI CISOs

The five threats that keep banking CISOs up at night.

Critical

API & open-banking abuse

UPI, AA, partner APIs and BBPS expose hundreds of business-logic endpoints. Bot armies and credential stuffing target them daily. Traditional WAFs miss it — you need API-aware protection with behavioural baselining.

Critical

Volumetric DDoS on customer-facing channels

Net banking, broker portals and customer apps are increasingly hit by multi-hundred-Gbps attacks timed to results day, market open or quarter-end. Origin-only protection cannot cope.

High

Insider exfiltration of customer data

Branch staff, contractors, third-party processors. PII, KYC documents, transaction history. DPDP penalties make this a board-level risk.

High

Ransomware via partner ecosystems

Vendors, sub-brokers, agents and IT outsourcers are the soft underbelly. Lateral movement from a compromised partner can cripple core banking.

High

RBI / SEBI 6-hour reporting clock

From incident detection to a written report on the regulator's desk: 6 hours. Without an instrumented SOC and pre-approved templates, this clock is unforgiving.

Emerging

GenAI in customer service & underwriting

Prompt injection, model poisoning, sensitive prompt logging. Your AI copilot is a new attack surface — and a new compliance surface.
